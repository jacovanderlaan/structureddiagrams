Patterns & anti-patterns

The shapes that work, and the ones that keep failing

A pattern is a shape you reach for instead of inventing one — a repeatable solution someone already paid to learn. An anti-pattern is the same thing in reverse: a shape that keeps being reached for and keeps failing. Naming both is what makes either usable, so they share a page. Filter by type to see them apart.
